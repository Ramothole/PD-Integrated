Who we are

Compliance that protects people, not just paperwork

PD Integrated SHEQ Solutions is a South African consulting business focused on helping organisations manage safety, health, environmental and quality risks, strengthen compliance, and improve operational performance.

We work with clients to understand their activities, applicable requirements and business priorities, then help develop and improve processes and documentation that can be applied in day-to-day operations. Compliance should help protect people, reduce risk, support continuity and improve business performance.

Owned by Duncan Mnisi and Phakamani Msibi.

Our Vision

To become a trusted SHEQ and ISO consulting partner in South Africa, recognised for practical solutions, professional service and a commitment to safer, more resilient and better-managed organisations.

Our Mission

To help organisations strengthen workplace safety, environmental responsibility, quality, business continuity and management system performance through consulting, internal audits, training and continual improvement.

Why work with us

Implementation, evidence and continual improvement

PD Integrated SHEQ Solutions aims to provide professional, reliable and client-focused consulting support. We seek to understand client expectations, agree clear deliverables, protect confidential information and identify opportunities for improvement.

Internal audits are planned and conducted against agreed criteria and within the auditor's competence. Where independence or impartiality is required, audit arrangements are established to avoid auditing one's own work or creating conflicts of interest.

  • Integrated support across safety, environment, quality, business continuity, information security and IT service management.
  • Practical documentation designed around real work activities and agreed requirements.
  • Client-focused services scaled to the organisation, project and risk profile.
  • Structured internal audit and corrective action support.
  • Emphasis on implementation and evidence, not documentation alone.
  • Flexible engagement options, including project-based work and ongoing support.
