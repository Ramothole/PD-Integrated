1. Who we are

PD Integrated SHEQ Solutions ("we", "us") is a South African SHEQ and ISO management system consulting business. We are the responsible party for personal information collected through this website.

2. What we collect

When you submit an enquiry we collect only what we need to respond: your name, company name, email address, telephone number, the service you are interested in and the details you include in your message.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare a quotation.
  • To communicate with you about the services you requested.
  • To keep records of enquiries and agreed work, where required.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

4. Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers who help us operate this website or deliver our services, and only where they protect it appropriately, or where the law requires us to.

5. Security and retention

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. We keep it only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for, or as required by law.

6. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to its processing, and lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact PD Integrated SHEQ Solutions using the details on our contact section.
